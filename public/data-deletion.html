Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 24 November 2026

HYTOMA Automation respects your privacy and provides a way to request deletion of personal information collected through WhatsApp enquiries and our customer management services.

1. How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your personal data, please send an email to:

Email: info@hytoma.com

Suggested subject: Personal Data Deletion Request

2. Information to Include

Please provide:

  • Your name, if previously provided to HYTOMA.
  • The WhatsApp number you used to contact HYTOMA.
  • A clear request to delete your personal information.

We may ask for reasonable verification to confirm that the request relates to your information. Please do not send passwords or one-time verification codes.

3. What Information May Be Deleted

Depending on your request and applicable law:

  • WhatsApp enquiry and conversation records.
  • Customer contact details.
  • Project and qualification information.
  • Sales follow-up records.
  • Marketing contact records where applicable.

4. Processing Your Request

HYTOMA will review your request, verify ownership where necessary, and respond with the outcome.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate record-keeping obligations. We will explain applicable limitations when responding to the request.

5. Stop Marketing Messages

If you only want to stop receiving promotional WhatsApp messages, you can contact us and request removal from our marketing contact list without requesting deletion of all your information.
