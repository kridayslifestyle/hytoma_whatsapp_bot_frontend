HYTOMA Automation ("HYTOMA", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and handle personal information when you contact us through WhatsApp or use our services.

We may use your information to:

HYTOMA uses the official WhatsApp Business Platform to communicate with customers. Some enquiries may initially be handled by automated responses before being reviewed by our team.

WhatsApp communications are also subject to Meta and WhatsApp's applicable privacy policies and terms.

We may share information with authorized HYTOMA team members and service providers who help us deliver our services, including WhatsApp messaging, infrastructure hosting, and customer management.

We do not sell customer personal information.

5. Data Security

We use access controls and reasonable technical safeguards designed to protect customer information against unauthorized access, alteration, and disclosure.

6. Data Retention

We retain customer information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, manage enquiries, maintain business records, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Where you have opted in to marketing communications, you can withdraw your consent by asking us to stop sending promotional messages or by contacting info@hytoma.com.
